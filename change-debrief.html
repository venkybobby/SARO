STORY-COV-001 — Observation-Gap (Coverage) Attestations

Change debrief · story 8/8 (final) · CISO differentiator P1 · stacked on story/STORY-MTR-001

1. Context

SARO observes AI outputs mirror-async, so it is sometimes blind — an adapter outage, a lag spike, a deploy window. Today that blindness is silent, which an examiner reads as either dishonesty or ignorance. COV-001 turns "when SARO wasn't watching" into first-class evidence: gap records with cause and bounds, a coverage %, and a measured maximum observation lag — converting mirror-async's weakness into a diligence artifact.

2. Intuition + scope

Each observation source emits checkpoints (last-observed watermark position + timestamp). When checkpoints stop advancing past the expected cadence, a gap opens (cause UNKNOWN until diagnosed); when they resume, it closes with duration + watermark delta. De-identified throughout (INV-2): positions and timestamps only, never observed content. SARO even confesses its own downtime (a retroactive SARO_OUTAGE gap on restart).

Owner-locked scope: this is the adapter-independent core. The live Bedrock-adapter heartbeat emission (STORY-406) doesn't exist yet, so AC-1's live wiring is deferred and documented — the checkpoint interface ships, and a future adapter just calls it.

5. Understanding check

Why record gaps at all instead of just reporting uptime %?
A bare % is unfalsifiable and reads as marketing. A gap record — {system, adapter, start, end, cause, watermark bounds} — is auditable evidence an examiner can inspect: it says exactly WHEN SARO was blind, for how long, and WHY. Disclosing gaps as evidence is the opposite of hiding them behind an aggregate; it's what turns mirror-async blindness from a liability into honesty.
How is a gap record kept PHI-free by construction?
The schema has no content column at all — only system_id/adapter_id identifiers, watermark positions (source-side offsets), and timestamps. A "watermark delta" expresses the range of missed source activity as positions/timestamps, never the missed content itself. There's nowhere for observed payload to land.
Why does SARO open a retroactive gap for its OWN downtime?
If SARO crashes, it wasn't observing — and a coverage claim that ignored its own outages would be dishonest. On restart, a checkpoint discontinuity opens a retroactive SARO_OUTAGE gap. The system confesses its downtime rather than letting the gap look like the source's fault (or vanish).
Why is AC-1's live wiring deferred, and is that a hole?
AC-1's heartbeat emission depends on STORY-406 (the Bedrock adapter contract), which doesn't exist — the story set stops at 404. Per an owner-locked decision, this story ships the adapter-INDEPENDENT core (the checkpoint interface + all gap/lag/report logic) and defers the live wiring. It's documented in the notes + story out-of-scope, so it's an honest, scoped deferral — a future adapter just calls record_checkpoint.
